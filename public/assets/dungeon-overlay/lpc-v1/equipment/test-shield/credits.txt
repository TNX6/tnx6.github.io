Universal LPC Crusader Shield

Exact source files:
- shield/crusader/bg/walk/crusader.png
- shield/crusader/fg/male/walk/crusader.png
- shield/crusader/bg/hurt/crusader.png
- shield/crusader/fg/male/hurt/crusader.png

Idle behavior:
- Crusader has no dedicated idle sheet.
- Each direction uses frame 1 (column 0) from its corresponding original walk
  row, copied unchanged into idle columns 0 and 1.
- Idle remains static; no walk animation is played.

Layer order:
- background: zPos 2, shield/crusader/bg
- male foreground: zPos 110, shield/crusader/fg/male

Authors:

Source:

Processing:
- Original pixels retained. Canonical coordinates are retained for back, left,
  right, and hurt.
- The complete front/down frames are translated +27px on x so the shield is
  held by the character-left hand opposite the sword.
- Transparent padding was added on the right to reach the existing LPC V1
  832-pixel sheet width.
- No redraw, resize, rotation, mirroring, recolor, or heater offsets.

Universal LPC Test Plate Armor
================================

This equipment-only test set combines the original male Plate torso, Plate arm armor,
and male Pauldrons layers. Original pixels and coordinates are preserved. The source
layers were alpha-composited and transparently padded to the 13-column prototype grid.

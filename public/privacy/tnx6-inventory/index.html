TNX6 Inventory — Privacy Notice

Last updated: 26 October 2026. The operator is TNX6, Twitch channel tnx_6. It covers the TNX6 Inventory Twitch Extension, its backend, and the TNX6 account and bot integrations required for its features. Twitch and independent services also have their own privacy notices.

Data and purposes

We process your Twitch ID, login, display name and avatar, and your linked TNX6 account to authenticate you and show your profile. Existing TNX6 data includes loyalty points, item ownership and quantities, XP, levels, badges, watch-time and check-in data used for profile progression.

Verified streak events include the account and channel IDs, count, event ID and share time. We retain highest progress, XP receipts and badge entitlements to calculate progression and prevent repeated awards. Item use, sale, gift and trade records include the parties, items, quantities, consideration, status, timestamps, request IDs and outcomes. A custom stream title you submit and another player affected by an item may be recorded. These records support execution, recovery, settlement and duplicate prevention.

We check channel ban/timeout status and expiry when available, and process information required for temporary moderator permissions. Recipient suggestions use registered TNX6 logins and, when authorized by the broadcaster, channel chat-connected account names. An explicit complete-name lookup can verify a Twitch account. Chat connection is not proof of video viewing. Suggestions are limited; they do not expose a complete Twitch directory or another user's entire inventory.

Authentication/authorization tokens, operation states, error codes and times are processed as required for operation and security. Twitch and hosting providers may process connection information such as IP addresses and browser information under their settings and notices. The current extension frontend has no advertising analytics integrations and does not request your Twitch password or payment card details.

Recipients and visibility

Processing involves Twitch, the TNX6 backend hosted on Cloudflare, and the channel operator's local systems, including Streamer.bot and OBS when an item needs them. Extension files are hosted by Twitch; this notice is part of the TNX6 website hosted on GitHub Pages.

Trade parties see the offer and the other party's relevant identity, not their entire inventory. Depending on the item, an account name or result may appear in channel chat, the stream title or a public stream effect. Some progression and badge data is already available in TNX6 community profiles and leaderboards. Necessary information may be disclosed in response to a binding legal request.

Storage and retention

The extension uses browser session storage for request recovery checkpoints and dismissed trade notices, not Twitch authorization tokens. Clearing it does not cancel server operations or erase their records. The separate TNX6 website uses essential login and OAuth verification cookies; its currently issued login session lasts 30 days. Twitch manages its own cookies and identity flow.

Account, balance, progression and transaction records remain while needed to operate the TNX6 account, settle operations, protect balances and prevent duplicate rewards. There is currently no single automatic deletion period for these records. A 30-day gap without a newly verified streak share resets the displayed streak, not the highest progress, XP history or earned badge entitlement. Hiding a trade notice after 10 seconds is a presentation change, not deletion. Successful chat-name snapshots currently have a 45-second server cache lifetime; this is not an account-record or infrastructure-log retention limit.

Your choices and requests

You can decline identity sharing or revoke extension access in Twitch settings. Account features require identity and may stop after revocation; revocation does not automatically delete the TNX6 account or its records. Contact the operator to request access, correction or deletion. Account ownership must be verified; never send a password or login code. Pending operations or another party's rights may need resolution first, and minimal settlement, anti-duplication or legally required records may need retention. There is currently no automatic full-account deletion button in the extension.

HTTPS and server-side identity and permission checks help protect data; no safeguard guarantees prevention of every incident. This notice will be updated when processing changes, with an updated date. It does not grant additional Twitch permissions.

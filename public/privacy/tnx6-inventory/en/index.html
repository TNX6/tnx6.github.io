This notice covers the TNX6 Inventory Twitch Extension and its connected TNX6 services. We use data needed to operate your account and inventory, execute requests and protect balances. We do not request your Twitch password or payment card details.

Data and purposes

  • Twitch identity and account-linking data: your ID, login, display name and avatar, to authenticate you and show your profile.
  • Items, points, XP, levels, badges, streaks, watch-time and check-in data, to display and synchronize progression.
  • Item-use, sale, gift and trade records, their parties and outcomes, and text you submit such as a stream title, to execute and settle requests and prevent duplication.
  • Suggested recipient names, including chat-connected names when authorized by the channel, restriction status, temporary permissions, and authentication and operational data, to provide features and enforce restrictions. Providers may also process connection data such as IP addresses and browser information for security and operation.

Sources are Twitch, your inputs and TNX6 records.

Sharing and visibility

Processing involves Twitch, Cloudflare, GitHub Pages and the channel operator's systems according to their roles. External services also have their own privacy notices.

Trade parties see necessary offer details, not the other party's entire inventory. Your name or an item's outcome may appear in chat or on stream, and progression may appear in community profiles and leaderboards. Necessary data may be disclosed in response to a binding legal request.

Storage and website metrics

The extension uses session storage to follow up requests; the website uses essential login cookies. The TNX6 website, including this page, uses Cloudflare Web Analytics for visit and page-performance metrics.

Retention

We retain account, progression and transaction data while needed to operate the service, settle operations, prevent duplication and meet legal obligations. There is currently no single automatic deletion period. Hiding messages or the expiry of a temporary feature does not delete its records.

Your choices and contact

You may decline identity sharing or revoke extension access in Twitch settings. Account features may stop; revocation does not automatically delete TNX6 data.

To request access, correction or deletion, email ddxdx240@gmail.com. We verify account ownership. Pending operations may need resolution, and minimal records may require retention for settlement, anti-duplication or legal obligations. Never send a password or login code.

We use technical safeguards; no protection is absolute. We update this notice's date when our practices change.
